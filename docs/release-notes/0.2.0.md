A big visual refresh for **Screen Playground**, plus on-screen keyboard shortcuts and many fixes.

## Download

Download **ScreenPlayground-0.2.0.dmg** below, open it, and drag Screen Playground into Applications (replace the old copy if asked).
- Requires macOS 15 or later.
- Runs on Apple silicon and Intel Macs.

## What's new

- **New light design with Liquid Glass.** On macOS 26 the toolbars, panels and timeline use Apple's Liquid Glass; on macOS 15 they use frosted glass. A calm, solid backdrop keeps the video front and centre.
- **Keyboard shortcuts on the video.** Turn on Shortcuts in the sidebar and shortcuts you pressed while recording (like ⌘C or ⇧⌘4) appear as keycaps in the preview and export.
- **Smoother feel.** Buttons respond when pressed, selections slide into place, panels fade between each other, and the Home screen and Export window animate gently. Keyboard actions stay instant, and Reduce Motion is respected.
- **Clearer playback controls.** The play button is green and turns into a red stop button while playing.

## Fixed

- The end of a recording is no longer cut off when the screen was still for the last few seconds.
- The playback bar no longer touches the timeline in smaller windows.
- The Export button keeps its colour while another window is in front.
- Top toolbar buttons are aligned, and the project title stays centred.
