The first public build of **Screen Playground**: a free, native macOS screen recorder that makes polished videos with no editing skills.

## Download

Download **ScreenPlayground-0.1.0.dmg** below, open it, and drag Screen Playground into Applications.
- Requires macOS 15 or later.
- Runs on Apple silicon and Intel Macs.
- Allow Screen Recording on first launch.

## What it does

- **Record** a display, a window or an area. Press ⌥⌘↩ anywhere to start or stop, with pause, restart and recording flags. Recordings survive crashes.
- **Auto-zoom** follows your clicks. You can tune the timing, aim zooms manually with a live preview, or use the Glass Loupe magnifier.
- **Smooth cursor** with motion blur, re-rendered sharp at any size, with click effects. Pick a Touch or Outline style if you prefer.
- **Make it look good** with wallpapers (including your Mac's own), gradients, padding, rounded corners and shadows, in 16:9, 9:16, 1:1, 4:3, 3:4 or 4:5.
- **Edit fast**:
  - split (C), ripple delete (X), trim by dragging;
  - per-clip speed and volume;
  - blur masks and highlights;
  - presets;
  - full undo.
- **Webcam overlay** or split-screen layouts, plus microphone and system audio, and background music.
- **Export** to MP4 (up to 4K60), GIF or ProRes, or copy straight to the clipboard to paste into Messages or Slack.

Free for everyone: no account, no watermark, no tracking.

## Fixed since the preview

- **Crash:** the app no longer quits when you delete the last segment of a clip.
- **Background music:** you can now hear it while previewing, and adding or removing it applies immediately.
- **Paused preview:** it now updates on every click and edit.
- **Timeline:** segments sit side by side with clear gaps, and trim handles only appear on hover. Zooms can't be dragged over each other.
- **Manual zoom:** the target picker shows the real frame and what the zoom will show, and the preview jumps into the zoom while you aim it.
- **Undo:** two separate changes to the same slider are now two separate undo steps.
